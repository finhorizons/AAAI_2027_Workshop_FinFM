## Hero artwork

`hero.png` was created for this website with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool, under the organizer's authorization, on 28 September 2026. It is conceptual art, not a scientific visualization or financial dataset.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: hero artwork for a sophisticated academic workshop website, FinFM, about foundation models and generative AI for finance. Create a premium editorial 3D macro sculpture of flowing luminous fine filaments, like hundreds of parallel financial signals, weaving into a single organic Möbius-like loop or toroidal flowing ribbon. It should feel mathematical, physical, sculptural and refined, with mint, ivory and pale acidic chartreuse fibers, satin/glass materials, very fine tactile grain and beautiful realistic soft lighting. Backdrop: solid very dark forest green (#102e25). Center one large asymmetrical loop diagonally, with generous negative space near the edges. Square composition, suitable for cropping inside a website's right-hand hero panel. High-end technology research brand art direction, quiet and confident, editorial not sci-fi. No text, letters, logos, icons, graphs, people, currency symbols, watermarks, neon blue, purple or fake UI. Render a finished bitmap artwork.
