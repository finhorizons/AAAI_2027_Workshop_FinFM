01The workshop

Beyond capability.
Toward reliability.

Foundation models and generative AI have moved from research prototypes to production systems in finance.

Yet the evidence behind these systems is fragile. Reported forecasting gains often fade under careful evaluation, and common practice remains exposed to look-ahead, survivorship and cost biases.

FinFM brings AI researchers, finance academics, practitioners and regulators together to establish how foundation models and generative AI for finance should be built, evaluated and validated.

Evidence before claims

Leakage-free benchmarks and reproducible, cost-aware evaluation.

Research meets practice

Open discussion across academia, industry, and financial institutions.

Resources for the community

A planned evaluation checklist and curated benchmark resources.

02Research directions

The questions
worth asking.

The topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following.

01

Models & representations

Learning the language of financial markets.

  • Financial LLMs and continued pretraining
  • Time-series and order-book foundation models
  • Multimodal models for filings, news and prices
  • Generative scenario generation and stress testing
02

Agents & reasoning

From information to grounded decisions.

  • LLM agents for research, trading, compliance and advice
  • Retrieval-augmented and tool-augmented reasoning
  • Guardrails, hallucination control and safety
  • Deployment in regulated settings: small vs. frontier models
03

Evaluation & trustworthiness

Making progress measurable and reliable.

  • Leakage-free benchmarks and cost-aware evaluation
  • Uncertainty and calibration
  • Interpretability for model risk management
  • Systemic risk and model-risk validation

03Call for papers

Bring your research.
Move the field forward.

Submissions must be prepared using the AAAI-27 template and anonymized for double-blind review. We accept two kinds of paper.

Track 01

Research papers

Novel methods, theory, or experiments. Eligible for the Best Paper Award.

Track 02

Benchmark papers

New benchmarks, datasets, or evaluation protocols. Eligible for the Best Benchmark Award.

Long papers
Up to 7 pages of technical content, with unlimited pages for references and appendices
Short papers
Up to 4 pages of technical content, with unlimited pages for references and appendices
Supplementary material
Allowed, but reviewers are not required to review it
Awards
Determined by the quality of the work, regardless of page length

Official submission portal. Submit your paper through the FinFM venue on OpenReview. An OpenReview account is required.

Review process. Submissions are planned to undergo double-blind review on OpenReview, with at least two reviews and an organizer meta-review per paper. Please prepare an anonymized manuscript.

Presentations & proceedings. All accepted papers are planned to be presented as posters, with selected oral presentations and a benchmark spotlight. The workshop is non-archival by default; an opt-in online proceedings volume is planned. Final arrangements will be announced.

Important dates

  1. Submissions open02 Oct 2026 00:00 AoE
  2. Paper submission deadline20 Nov 2026 23:59 AoE
  3. Author notification02 Dec 2026
  4. Workshop22 or 23 Feb 2027 Final day to be confirmed

Submission times use Anywhere on Earth (AoE, UTC−12). The workshop day is still to be confirmed. See the tentative program.
